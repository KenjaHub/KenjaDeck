# KenjaDeck

**Build decks, present, and ask the AI about your slides.** A clean, fast, private-by-default
presentation app for Mac — themes, layouts, shapes, charts, presenter tools, plus a built-in
read-only AI assistant that reads your deck over an endpoint **you** configure.

*Your deck. Your model. Your machine.*

**[App repository](https://github.com/koihuang/KenjaDeck) · [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) · [Support](https://github.com/koihuang/KenjaDeck/issues)**

**Coming soon to the Mac App Store.**

</div>

---

KenjaDeck is a native macOS presentation app: masters and layouts, 150+ preset shapes, tables
and charts, transitions and animations, PowerPoint file import/export, full-screen presenting —
and an AI assistant that answers questions about the open deck through read-only tools, talking
to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you bring (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, or a local Ollama/LM
Studio). No accounts, no telemetry, no tracking; decks never leave your machine.

> "Kenja" is Japanese for *sage* — the wise one. Building smarter software is the mission.
