01

From blank slide to applause

Build it, ask about it, present it, share it — one quiet, native Mac app.

STEP 01 · BUILD

Slides that come together in minutes

Start from themes and slide layouts, or slide masters with placeholders that inherit everything. A real slide sorter with sections, 150+ preset shapes, tables and charts — and keyboard-driven editing throughout. A full dark theme for late-night decks.

STEP 02 · ASK

An AI assistant that reads along

Open the AI pane and ask about any slide — it answers from what it actually inspected, with every read shown as a trace line. Read-only by design: it never edits, saves or closes your file. Bring your own model: any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, your key stays on your Mac. The app is fully functional without it.

STEP 03 · PRESENT

Present with confidence

F5 plays from the start, Shift+F5 from the current slide. Transitions — including Morph — entrance, emphasis and exit animations, motion paths and by-paragraph builds, with a live preview. Presenter view, pen, rehearse timings and media playback on stage.

STEP 04 · SHARE

PowerPoint files welcome

Open .pptx presentations — also .potx templates and .ppsx shows — and save them back. A native .kenjadeck format when you want it, PDF export (slides, notes pages, handouts with selectable text), PNG/JPEG images and plain-text outlines. AutoRecover keeps unsaved work safe.

04

Questions, answered

Does KenjaDeck upload my presentations anywhere?

No. Creating, editing, rendering, exporting and presenting all run locally on your Mac. The app initiates no network traffic of its own — the only traffic ever sent is an AI-chat request that you triggered, delivered straight to the endpoint you configured. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Can the AI assistant edit my deck?

No. The assistant is read-only by design: it answers questions about the open deck through a whitelist of four inspection tools — deck overview, slide contents, a rendered slide, the command catalogue — and every read shows as a trace line in the transcript. It never edits, saves or closes your file, and the conversation lives in memory only.

Which AI models can I use?

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure yourself: OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, or a local server such as Ollama or LM Studio. Your API key is stored on your Mac and sent only to the base URL you configured, only when you send a message. The app is fully functional without any AI.

Can KenjaDeck open my PowerPoint files?

Yes. KenjaDeck opens .pptx presentations — also .potx templates and .ppsx shows — and saves them back. It also has a native .kenjadeck format and exports PDF (slides, notes pages, handouts with selectable text), PNG/JPEG images and plain-text outlines.

Does it support transitions and animations?

Yes — transitions including Morph, entrance, emphasis and exit animations, motion paths and by-paragraph builds, with a live preview. Presenting adds a full-screen slide show, presenter view, pen, rehearse timings and media playback.

What are the system requirements?

KenjaDeck runs on macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon Macs.
